## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| phone browser -> presign/analyze/view routes | Signed-in but untrusted users request write and read access |
| phone browser -> R2 | Direct upload with a presigned URL |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-01-12-01 | Denial of service / Tampering | Presign abuse for free storage | medium | mitigate | Session required; MIME allowlist matched to kind; 30 MB photo / 100 MB video caps; server-generated keys; 60 uploads per user per 24 h (429) |
| T-01-12-02 | Elevation of privilege | IDOR on analyze and view | high | mitigate | Both routes load the row and return 404 unless `row.userId === actor.id` |
| T-01-12-03 | Information disclosure | Owner's location in committed evidence | medium | mitigate | Evidence holds presence flags, accuracy and distances only; acceptance greps matrix.json for coordinate keys |
| T-01-12-04 | Tampering | Malicious media exploiting sharp/exifr/ffprobe | medium | mitigate | Size caps, `execFile` with fixed args and a 20 s timeout, temp files deleted in `finally`, no shell; residual parser risk accepted for this private diagnostic page until Phase 4 moves analysis to the worker |
| T-01-12-05 | Information disclosure | Signed URL leakage | low | mitigate | 300 s expiry; view route requires ownership; signed URLs never stored in evidence |
| T-01-12-06 | Information disclosure | Spike objects kept in the bucket after the phase | low | accept | Bucket is private and reachable only by signed URL; objects are kept for end-of-phase UAT and Phase 4 calibration, and deletion is recorded as a follow-up in SPIKES.md |
